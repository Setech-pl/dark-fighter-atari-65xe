# Rejected full-playfield ANTIC 2 spike

An experimental branch evaluated replacing the ANTIC 4 gameplay playfield with
ANTIC 2 text. The experiment improved simple character-cell throughput but did
not preserve the accepted local colour ownership, capital-hull detail, weapon
readability, and overlay composition within the available charset and timing
envelope.

The spike was rejected. Current gameplay keeps the fixed ANTIC 2 HUD/divider
and the 22-row ANTIC 4 playfield. No address, payload, glyph count, or timing
value from the experiment is a current constraint.
